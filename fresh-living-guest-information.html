Guest Information

​​​Thank you for joining us on Fresh Living! We're excited to have you on the show. Please read these instructions as they answer many common questions.

BEFORE YOUR APPEARANCE

Please complete and submit the Guest Segment Information form at least two business days before your scheduled segment. If we don't receive your completed form by the deadline, your segment may need to be rescheduled to allow adequate production time.

 TIMETABLE

ARRIVAL TIME

Please arrive by 9:45AM so we have time to prepare and fit your microphone.


RECORDING TIME

10:00AM to 11:00AM

 STUDIO LOCATION

Our studio is in the Wells Fargo Building at:
299 South Main Street Suite 150
Salt Lake City, UT 84111

 PARKING & TRANSIT

There is an attached parking garage directly east of the Wells Fargo Building; garage entrance is on 300 South. Street parking is also available or the Trax Gallivan Plaza Station is directly outside the building. Parking validation is not available.

 ENTRY INFORMATION

KUTV is located at the red lobby doors on the west side of the main floor lobby. The doors are locked for security reasons. Please use the phone located outside the doors and dial 2 when prompted, or call our newsroom directly at 801-839-1333 and someone will let you in.

 WATCH YOUR SEGMENT

Your Fresh Living segment will be published on KUTV.com under Features → Fresh Living shortly after it airs.
